Data Privacy

Account Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play User Data guidelines, PiklrPH players can request the immediate and permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

What data is deleted:

  • Player profile (name, nickname, contact number, avatar)
  • Skill & DUPR match history logs
  • Court queue reservations & active bookings
  • Chat, partner invites, and forum submissions

How to Delete:

Method 1 (In-App Instant): Open PiklrPH → Go to Profile → Tap Delete Account & Data.
Method 2 (Email Support): Send an email to support@piklr.ph with your registered phone number. We process requests within 48 hours.
